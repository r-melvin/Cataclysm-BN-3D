02. Patterns from comparable games

What other games do, whether it fits Bright Nights 3D, and the evidence grade. Raw findings with URLs are in research/. Grades: V = the research agent fetched the page and I spot-checked it or the claim is corroborated by two sources; S = search snippet only; U = unverified or from memory. Do not cite S/U items as researched facts.

Fit column: Adopt, Adapt (fits with a stated change), Reject (does not fit, with the reason). "Turn-based" matters: BN has no real time, so any pattern that relies on timing (fading HUDs, hold-progress rings tied to seconds) has to be re-based on turns or on wall-clock while waiting for input.

One contextual interact verb with a promptCommon in FPS/survival (U as a general pattern; prompt text verified in STALKER 2 complaint "the F menu dissapears", V)Look at a thing, see "E: Open", press E.AdoptReplaces most of BN's dedicated verb keys (open, close, smash, examine, pick up, talk, board). Hold-to-confirm with one radial fillUnity docs (dated), general (S)Long-press for destructive or slow actions.AdaptOnly for a few actions (disassemble, drop all, butcher). Patterns rejected outright

  • Floating/pinnable windows (Zomboid): mouse-first, breaks focus navigation.
  • Spatial grid inventory (DayZ, RE4): game-rule change, large UI, poor with text scaling.
  • Fully diegetic HUD (Metro): slower to read, not screen-reader friendly, and BN is turn-based so "glance at your watch" has no cost anyway.
  • Marker-following navigation (Skyrim-style objective arrow to every target): prior art and this research agree it replaces looking. Mission target on the compass only.
  • Timed popups (BG3 complaint): nothing informational disappears on a timer; only the "Entering: place" notice fades, and it is also logged.
  • Two-stage analog trigger gestures (BG3): not mappable to keyboard, hard to make accessible.
